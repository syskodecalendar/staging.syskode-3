# Dedicated industry pages

All 16 industry cards now have a Know more link to a dedicated industry page. The original Industries layout and content remain unchanged. Each page includes a sector overview, six relevant solution areas with selectable sticky imagery, a side menu, delivery content over photography, project options, deliverables, FAQs, enquiry draft and related services.

Solutions describe potential scope to be agreed in a proposal. They do not assert client results, certification or regulatory approval. Images are illustrative.
