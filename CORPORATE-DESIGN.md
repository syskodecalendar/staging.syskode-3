# Syskode — service and industry inner pages

This version keeps all existing main-page designs. The only change to the existing Industries page is a Know more link on each of its 16 cards. Other main pages and existing supporting pages are retained.

The 24 service inner pages now have a side section menu and a sticky capability photograph that changes with each selection. Business applications and Connected experience sections have been removed. Discover, Design, Build and Launch display their content over a photograph with a contrast layer.

All 16 industries now have dedicated pages with relevant sector content and the same navigation, selectable imagery and delivery layout. Each includes six solution areas, project options, deliverables, FAQs, an enquiry draft and links to related services.

Small screens use an expandable section menu. Images flow with the page on mobile to preserve reading space. Existing enquiry and form behaviours remain unchanged.

The original entry point and runtime are retained. Photography is illustrative; sources are listed in SERVICE-IMAGE-CREDITS.md.
