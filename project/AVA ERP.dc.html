Questions about AVA ERP.

Is AVA ERP ready for ZATCA e-invoicing?+

Yes. AVA ERP is ready for Saudi ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing, as well as VAT across the GCC.

Can we start with only a few modules?+

Yes. AVA is modular, so you can start with your core needs, such as finance and inventory, and add modules as you grow.

Can AVA be customised to our processes?+

Yes. Modules, workflows, reports and dashboards are configured to the way your business works.

Does AVA work with our existing systems?+

Yes. AVA has an API-first architecture and connects to your existing applications, banks and online stores.

Does AVA support multiple companies and currencies?+

Yes. AVA supports multiple companies, local currencies and global tax rules from one system.

Is AVA a cloud ERP?+

AVA ERP is cloud ready. Talk to our team about the deployment that best fits your business.
