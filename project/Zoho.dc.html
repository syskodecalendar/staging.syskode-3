Zoho questions we hear most.

Can’t find your answer? Ask our Zoho team.

Is Syskode an official Zoho partner?+

Yes. Syskode Technologies is a Zoho Authorized Partner based in Bahrain, serving clients across the GCC, and is listed in Zoho’s official partner directory.

Which Zoho products do you implement?+

All of them. We implement every Zoho product, from single apps such as Zoho CRM, Books or Desk to complete Zoho One rollouts, plus custom apps on Zoho Creator.

Is Zoho Books VAT-compliant in Bahrain?+

Zoho Books supports VAT for GCC countries, including Bahrain. We set up your tax rates, VAT registration details and VAT return reports as part of the implementation.

Does Zoho support ZATCA e-invoicing in Saudi Arabia?+

Yes. The Saudi edition of Zoho Books supports ZATCA e-invoicing, including Phase 2 integration. We handle the setup and testing for companies with Saudi entities.

Is Zoho Payroll available in Bahrain?+

Yes. Zoho Payroll has a Bahrain edition, as well as editions for Saudi Arabia, the UAE and other GCC countries.

Can you move our data from Excel or our current system?+

Yes. We clean, map and migrate customers, suppliers, products, opening balances and history, and run test migrations before go-live.

Can Zoho work in Arabic?+

Many Zoho apps offer an Arabic interface, and Zoho Books supports Arabic and bilingual invoices. We also train your teams in Arabic or English.

How long does a Zoho implementation take?+

It depends on the apps and the amount of custom work. A focused CRM or Books setup is much shorter than a full ERP. After the discovery workshop you get a fixed scope, timeline and price.

Do you provide support after go-live?+

Yes. Our managed services plans give you ongoing support and improvements for a fixed monthly fee.
