Choose the right kind of agent.

Most businesses start with one type and add more as results come in. Every type shares the same rules, approvals and audit trail.

Chat agents

Text conversations on your website, WordPress site, WhatsApp and mobile app, in Arabic and English.

Best for: customer service, bookings, FAQs

Voice agents

Answer and make phone calls in Gulf Arabic and English, with transcripts and summaries for every call.

Best for: reception, appointments, reminders

Avatar agents

A face and a voice for kiosks, lobby screens and websites, like the demo above, branded to your business.

Best for: showrooms, hotels, service centres

Copilots

Assistants inside the tools your team already uses, such as AVA ERP, Microsoft Teams and Outlook.

Best for: finance, HR, operations

Back-office agents

Work behind the scenes on documents, emails and data, and ask a person before anything important.

Best for: invoices, onboarding, reporting

Multi-agent teams

Specialist agents that plan, check each other’s work and hand off tasks between them.

Best for: tenders, research, complex cases
