Engineering tomorrow’s enterprise.

Syskode Technologies W.L.L. is a technology company headquartered in Manama, Kingdom of Bahrain. We help organisations modernise how they work, from AI agents and process automation to enterprise software, ERP and immersive 3D experiences.

We are a Claude automation practice and an official Lovable partner, the team behind AVA ERP, and a three-time winner of the Bahrain Tech Award. Our clients range from government entities and national sports bodies to manufacturers, airports and fast-growing businesses.

Every project is designed, built and supported by our own teams, so the people who plan your system are the people who look after it.
