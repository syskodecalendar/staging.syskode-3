Overview

Make security part of how your systems operate.

Syskode helps teams assess application and infrastructure security needs, prioritise practical improvements and strengthen operational controls. The work starts with the systems, data and users in scope.

Security work should result in actions that the business can prioritise and maintain. We establish the authorised scope, identify important information and review relevant access, configuration and application controls. Findings are presented with context so the responsible teams understand their significance and the recommended next step. Remediation can then be planned and checked in stages. Ongoing readiness also depends on ownership, patching, logging and a usable escalation process.
